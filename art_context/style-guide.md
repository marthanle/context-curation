# My Style Guide
My aesthetic is soft, feminine, and editorially minimal. I like work that feels calm and intentional, like a page from a design magazine.
Palette:
- Matcha green #A3B18A
- Deep tea green #588157
- Warm cream #F5F0E6
- Soft blush #E8C7C1
- Ink #3A3A3A for text only
Composition: lots of empty space, one clear focal point, things placed slightly off center.
Shapes: soft organic forms, circles that feel hand drawn, thin lines, subtle grain or paper texture.
Type: small serif type (Georgia is fine), lowercase, generous letter spacing, tucked in a corner.
Motion: slow and quiet. Nothing should flash or move fast.
Objects should be recognizable at a glance. A tea bowl should be seen from the side, slightly from above, with smooth gently wobbly edges, not jagged or spiky. Steam rises softly from the top of the bowl.
