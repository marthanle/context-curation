# Art Brief
Theme: a slow morning matcha ritual.
Make a generative poster in p5.js, global mode, canvas 800 x 1000.
It must be interactive for a live demo:
- Moving the mouse gently changes the composition
- Clicking regenerates a new variation
- Pressing S saves the canvas as a PNG
Use only p5.js. No external images or fonts. A short title on the poster is allowed.
